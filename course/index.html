Free Book Writing & Publishing Course — Quillvibe Academy

Learn how to draft a book, organize chapters and story bibles, proofread with copyediting symbols, design print and eBook covers, typeset print interiors, and publish EPUB 3 and Print PDF editions—free, offline-ready, and with no account required.

Course Curriculum (10 Modules, 31 Lessons)

  1. Desk Setup, Workspace Layouts & Multi-Window Books — Create your book identity, switch between Focused, Editorial, and Full Studio layouts, resize columns, and work across tabs or windows.
  2. Drafting Scenes, Right-Click Tools & Immersive Focus — Organize parts, chapters, scenes, and snapshots, use right-click formatting, and write on Ivory, Parchment, or Obsidian paper.
  3. Story Architecture: Corkboard, Outline, Bible & Graph — Reorder scenes on the Corkboard, filter pacing in Outline, build Characters/Locations/Lore in Story Bible, and map arcs on Story Graph.
  4. Editorial Copyediting: Proofread Markup & Revision Diffs — Mark drafts with 15 copyediting proofreading symbols, toggle Clean Preview, and compare snapshots word by word.
  5. Interior Print Typesetting & Custom Typography — Configure trim sizes, margins, drop caps, custom .ttf/.otf fonts, front matter, Table of Contents, and export Print PDF and EPUB 3.
  6. Cover Studio: Vector Shapes, Gradients & Wraps — Design multi-layer Front, Spine, Back, and Dust Jacket Flap covers with vector shapes, gradients, and alignment guides.
  7. Author Sealing, Secure LAN/Bluetooth/Enterprise Sync & Release — Seal manuscripts with Author Keys and Work Copyright, run preflight checks, and share drafts offline.
  8. Protected File Locks (QVBX), Passwords & Safety Checks — Protect .qv book workspaces with optional passwords and import older archives safely.
  9. Forever Author Keys, Team Permissions & Commercial Releases — Bind permanent pen-name keys, pair verified co-authors, and configure commercial release details.
  10. Desktop App Extras (Windows, macOS & Linux) — Use fullscreen focus mode, stacked book tabs, multi-window editing, Wi-Fi sync, Bluetooth transfer, and file associations.
