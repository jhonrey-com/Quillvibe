Every stage of writing a book, kept in one calm place.

Most authors split a single book across five separate apps: one for scenes, another for character sheets, spreadsheets for outlines, design tools for covers, and converters for EPUB or Print PDF. Quillvibe brings drafting, worldbuilding, proofreading, layout, and cover design into one free, open-source workspace right in your browser.

Why writers move to Quillvibe

Designed specifically for long-form books rather than short blog posts, chat threads, or generic note wikis.

No more app switching

Switch between Focused drafting, Editorial corkboards, and Full Studio without moving chapters out of your manuscript or reformatting later.

15 traditional copyediting marks

Annotate drafts right on the page with standard proofreading symbols, toggle Clean Preview to inspect changes, and lock finished chapters against accidental edits.

Local .qv files, no lock-in

Your manuscripts stay on your own device in password-capable .qv archives with no cloud lock-in, no subscription paywalls, and open-source code under the MIT License.

Inside the Studio

Every tool needed to take an idea from first outline to print-ready manuscript and cover.

Manuscript Workspace: Drafting, Split View & Snapshots

Organize parts, chapters, and scenes in the Binder. Compare snapshots side by side, track daily word-sprint goals, insert wrapped illustrations, and import existing .docx, .md, or .rtf drafts cleanly.

Story Bible & Graph: Connected Characters, Places & Plotlines

Link entries automatically from prose via [[wikilinks]] and #tags. Inspect structure on a 90-degree orthogonal Storyboard or switch to a live Force Graph to trace character relationships across chapters.

Interior Typeset: Trade Trim Layout, EPUB 3 & Print PDF

Preview standard trade trim sizes (6"×9", 5.5"×8.5", 5"×8", 8.5"×11") with chapter typography, drop caps, front/back matter, custom uploaded fonts, and clean export packages.

Book Cover Studio: Front, Spine, Back & Dust-Jacket Wrap

Lay out shapes, ornaments, gradients, and custom typography with magnetic alignment guides, undo/redo history, and export crisp 1x/2x/3x PNG, JPEG, Print PDF, or Vector SVG wraps.
